# 🐉 Realm of Thrones Cheats (RoT 7.1)

A feature-rich, high-performance cheat suite designed specifically for **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord** running the **Realm of Thrones (RoT 7.1)** total conversion mod.

Featuring dedicated dragon spawning, god mode, one-hit kill with shield piercing, unlimited ammo, customizable movement & mount speed multipliers, unlimited carrying capacity, one-click legendary Valyrian steel weapons, infinite smithing stamina, companion skill editors, and full **Mod Configuration Menu (MCM v5)** integration.

## ⚡ Quick Access Methods

You can access the cheat suite using any of the following 4 convenient methods:

### 1. ⚙️ Mod Configuration Menu (MCM v5) — *Recommended*
Navigate to **Main Menu** or pause in-game (**Esc**) &rarr; **Options** &rarr; **Mod Options** &rarr; **`Realm of Thrones Cheats (v7.1)`**:
- Toggle cheats (God Mode, Unlimited Ammo, One-Hit Kill, Infinite Stamina, Unlimited Weight) with clean switches.
- Adjust sliders for Player, Squad, and Horseback speed.
- Click interactive action buttons to instantly spawn dragons, heal parties, grant wealth, unlock crafting parts, or max skills.

### 2. ⌨️ Global Hotkeys
Press either hotkey anywhere in-game (on the campaign map, inside settlements, or even in the middle of battle):
- **`F10`**
- **`Ctrl + Shift + C`**

### 3. ⛺ Settlement / Town / Camp Menu
When entering any town, castle, village, or opening the campaign camp menu, click the dedicated menu option:
- **`Realm of Thrones Cheats`**

### 4. 💻 Developer Console Commands (`Alt + ~`)
Open the Bannerlord developer console by pressing `Alt + ~` and use the following commands:
| Command | Description |
|---|---|
| `rotcheats.menu` | Opens the full interactive cheat inquiry menu |
| `rotcheats.godmode` | Toggles God Mode (Invulnerability + Auto-Heal) |
| `rotcheats.onehit` | Toggles One-Hit Kill (Shield & Guard Piercing) |
| `rotcheats.weight` | Toggles Unlimited Inventory Weight (10,000,000 capacity) |
| `rotcheats.speed <player> [squad] [mount]` | Sets movement speed multipliers (e.g. `rotcheats.speed 2.0 1.5 2.0`) |
| `rotcheats.dragon [id]` | Spawns and auto-equips a specific dragon (default: `dragon_black`) |
| `rotcheats.all_dragons` | Adds 1 of every dragon variant + 5 armored saddles to party inventory |
| `rotcheats.weapons` | Adds all 28 legendary Valyrian steel & unique weapons |
| `rotcheats.maxskills` | Sets all 18 skills to 300 for the Main Hero |

## ✨ Features Overview

### 🐉 1. Realm of Thrones Dragons
- **Instant Spawn & Auto-Equip**: Spawns your chosen dragon directly into your party inventory and immediately equips it into your active mount slot:
  - **Drogon** (Ground & Flying variants)
  - **Rhaegal** (Ground & Flying variants)
  - **Viserion** (Ground & Flying variants)
  - **Draeghar** (Ground & Flying variants)
  - **Gold Dragon** (Flying)
  - **Sea Smoke** (Flying)
- **Armored Dragon Saddle**: Spawns and equips the heavy armored saddle designed for dragons.
- **⭐ Spawn All Dragons Bundle**: Instantly adds 1 of every ground and flying dragon plus 5 armored saddles with a single click.

### 🛡️ 2. Combat & Invulnerability
- **Hero & Dragon God Mode**: Intercepts all incoming damage via Harmony prefix and mission tick enforcement, keeping you and your mount at 100% health.
- **Party God Mode**: Protects all allied troops and companions in your squad from taking battle damage.
- **One-Hit Kill (Shield & Guard Piercing)**: Any hit you or your soldiers land on an enemy deals 99,999 lethal damage, completely bypassing shields, parries, and blocks. *Enemies never receive this bonus.*
- **Unlimited Ammo**: Continuously tops off all arrow quivers, crossbow bolts, and throwing weapons in real-time during battles.
- **Instant Full Heal**: Restores you, your dragon, and your entire army to full health immediately.
- **Defeat All Enemies**: Instantly routes/eliminates all active enemy combatants in the current mission.

### 🏃 3. Movement & Mount Speed Multipliers
- **Player Speed Multiplier**: Configurable on-foot movement speed (1.0x to 5.0x).
- **Squad Speed Multiplier**: Configurable on-foot movement speed for friendly soldiers (1.0x to 5.0x).
- **Horseback & Mount Speed Multiplier**: Configurable riding speed and maneuverability for mounts and dragons (1.0x to 5.0x).

### ⚔️ 4. Inventory, Items & Economy
- **Unlimited Weight Limit**: Sets party carrying capacity to 10,000,000 and completely removes overburdened speed penalties.
- **In-Game Item Spawner**: Opens the native inventory transfer screen loaded with 100x of **every item in the game** (Weapons, Armor, Mounts, Goods, Crafting materials).
- **All 28 RoT Legendary Valyrian & Unique Weapons**: Adds Longclaw, Ice, Dark Sister, Blackfyre, Heartsbane, Oathkeeper, Widow's Wail, Dawn, Lightbringer, White Walker Sword, Needle, Brightroar, Lamentation, Lady Forlorn, Red Rain, Nightfall, and more.

### ⚒️ 5. Crafting & Smithing Stamina
- **Infinite Smithing Stamina**: Eliminates stamina depletion for refining, smelting, and weapon forging, keeping stamina locked at 100%.

## 📥 Installation & Load Order

### 1. Manual Installation
1. Compile the mod using `build.ps1` or download the compiled release.
2. Place the `ROT-Cheats` folder into your Bannerlord `Modules` directory:
   ```text
   ...\Mount & Blade II Bannerlord\Modules\ROT-Cheats
   ```
   The module folder structure should look like:
   ```text
   Modules/
   └── ROT-Cheats/
       ├── SubModule.xml
       └── bin/
           └── Win64_Shipping_Client/
               └── ROTCheats.dll
   ```

### 2. Recommended Load Order
In the Bannerlord Launcher, Novus Launcher, or BLSE, position `ROT-Cheats` **after** all RoT modules:

```text
1.  Bannerlord.Harmony
2.  Bannerlord.ButterLib (if installed)
3.  Bannerlord.UIExtenderEx (if installed)
4.  Bannerlord.MBOptionScreen (MCM v5)
5.  Native
6.  SandBoxCore
7.  Sandbox
8.  StoryMode
9.  CustomBattle
10. BirthAndDeath
11. ROT-Core
12. ROT-Content
13. ROT-Map
14. ROT-Dragon
```

## 🛠️ Building From Source

A turnkey build script is included that compiles the C# source code and directly deploys the module to your Bannerlord installation:

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\build.ps1
```

The script:
- Compiles all C# files under `Source/` using the 64-bit .NET compiler (`csc.exe`).
- Links against your game assemblies, Harmony, MCM, and RoT modules.
- Deploys `ROTCheats.dll` and `SubModule.xml` directly into `Modules/ROT-Cheats/bin/Win64_Shipping_Client/`.
